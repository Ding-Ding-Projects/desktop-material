shieldPatterns run with linear-time matching, bounded in time and size, so no pattern can hang the app. That is why lookaround and backreferences are not offered: they are shown struck through with the reason instead of being hidden.

schoolHow regex works

Literal text matches itselfpush finds every command with those four letters in a row.
Anchors pin the position^Push matches only at the start, main$ only at the end.
Classes match one character\d is a digit, \w a word character, [a-f] a range, . anything.
Quantifiers repeat* zero or more, + one or more, ? optional, {2,4} a counted range.
Groups and alternatives(pull|push) matches either word, and the group can take a quantifier.
Escape special characters\. matches a real dot. The builder escapes text you paste when you ask it to.
